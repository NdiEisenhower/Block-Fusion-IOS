No Account Required in Block Rush 2K

Block Rush 2K does not require or allow users to create an account. All gameplay progress (high scores, coin balances, unlocked worlds) is stored locally on your device or linked to anonymous diagnostic identifiers.

Option 1: Delete Local Data Immediately

Since your game data is stored directly on your phone or browser, you can erase it instantly without waiting for customer support:

1

On Android Device:

Open Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Block Rush 2K ➔ Storage & Cache ➔ Tap "Clear Storage" and "Clear Cache".

2

In Web Browser:

Click your browser settings ➔ Clear Browsing Data ➔ Select "Cookies and site data" (or open Settings in the game and use the Reset option).

3

Reset Advertising ID (Optional):

On your Android device, go to Settings ➔ Google ➔ All Services ➔ Ads ➔ Tap "Delete Advertising ID" to sever any advertising profile.
