1. Introduction

Welcome to Block Rush 2K ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile application on Google Play, our web-based game application, and any related services (collectively referred to as the "Service").

We believe in privacy by design. By playing or using Block Rush 2K, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this policy. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at the email provided at the bottom of this page.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver, maintain, and enhance your gaming experience:

A. No Account Creation Required

Block Rush 2K does not require or allow users to create an account. All players enjoy the game as guests without creating a profile, logging in, or providing personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or password.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Game Progress & Local Storage: High scores, coins, unlocked worlds, challenge streaks, and game settings are saved locally on your device (via browser localStorage or Android SharedPreferences).
  • Device & Usage Data: Operating system version, device model, language preference, and anonymous in-game interactions (e.g. levels played, puzzle merges completed) via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Logs: Stack traces, operating system state, and crash occurrences via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and performance bottlenecks.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may use standard device advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AD_ID) to deliver ads and measure ad performance in accordance with Google's policies.

C. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases (such as coin packs or ad removal) are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card numbers or financial account information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To save and synchronize your puzzle progress, trophies, and coin balances across sessions.
  • To provide competitive features such as global and daily challenge leaderboards.
  • To monitor, debug, and optimize application performance, responsiveness, and frame rates.
  • To deliver non-intrusive advertisements that support the free version of the game.
  • To fulfill customer support requests and address technical issues.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We take the security of your data seriously and enforce modern security protocols:

  • All network communication between your device, Firebase services, and game APIs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Local game saves are stored within secure application-private sandboxes on Android.
  • Access to cloud databases is protected by strict Firebase Security Rules that prevent unauthorized data access.
Safety Reminder: While we implement rigorous technical safeguards, please protect your device and Google credentials to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect your rights to control your personal and device data:

  • No Account Deletion Needed: Because Block Rush 2K does not allow or support user accounts, there is no online profile or password-protected account maintained on our servers.
  • Reset Local Gameplay Data: You can wipe all local progress, coins, and high scores at any time by clearing your device's app storage (Android: Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Block Rush 2K ➔ Storage ➔ Clear Storage) or clearing your browser cookies/cache.
  • Request Associated Data Deletion: If you wish to request the deletion of any associated telemetry, crash logs, or advertising identifiers, you can submit a formal request via our dedicated deletion page:
Request Associated Data Deletion Page

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Block Rush 2K is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information from our records.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates, new features, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
